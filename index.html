FRACTAL-6

// BI-ANNUAL LAUNCH //

Fractal-6 is a platform for global ARG puzzles. We create stories to link the real world and the speculative world together, and it is your job to put the pieces together and solve these puzzles.

THE BI-ANNUAL INTERCEPT

Every six months, we release a new scenario. Our aim is to provide members with an evolving story that requires collaboration, cryptography, and logic to solve.

Members are sent physical artifacts and provided with websites to help solve the current puzzle. Once the six-month window expires, the data is archived and a new mystery begins.

This is a community built on the enjoyment of solving. Join the intercept.
