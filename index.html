// BI-ANNUAL LAUNCH //

Fractal-6 is a platform for global ARG puzzles. We create stories to link the real world and the speculative world together, and it is your job to put the pieces together and solve these puzzles.

THE BI-ANNUAL INTERCEPT

Every six months, we release a new scenario into the digital and physical world. Our aim is to provide participants with an evolving story that requires cross-media collaboration, cryptography, and logic to navigate.

Members are sent physical artifacts and provided with secure digital entry points to assist in the resolution of the current objective. Each scenario is a closed loop. Once the six-month window expires, the data is archived and a new mystery begins its approach.

This is a community built on the thrill of the solve. Join the intercept.
